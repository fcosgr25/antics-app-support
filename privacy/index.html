Privacy policy

Your game night is not an ad profile.

ANTICS can be played without an account. We do not sell personal information, run advertising, or use analytics or cross-app tracking in version 1.0.

Effective and last updated: November 10, 2026

Short version: Guest play stays on your device. If you choose to sign in, the account, profile, social connections, scores, daily results, and progress described below are stored with Supabase so they can sync and power rankings. You can delete the account and its synced data from ANTICS settings.

What this policy covers

This policy describes information handled by the ANTICS mobile app and its account service. “ANTICS,” “we,” and “us” refer to the developer of ANTICS. It does not replace the separate privacy policies of Apple, Google, Supabase, GitHub, or any app you choose from the iOS share sheet.

Information that stays on your device

You may use ANTICS as a guest. The app stores gameplay and preferences locally so it can work offline. Local information may include:

  • Your local player name, utility rosters, pinned games, preferences, and haptic setting.
  • Scores, personal bests, daily progress, play history, and achievements.
  • Drafts or names you enter for local party games and utilities.

This local information is not sent to ANTICS merely because you enter it. You can remove it with Reset local data in Settings or by deleting the app. If you later sign in and choose to use sync, eligible guest progress and scores may be imported into your account.

Account information is linked to your ANTICS account because that link is necessary for syncing, security, rankings, friend features, and deletion. ANTICS does not use this information to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Service providers

  • Supabase provides authentication, database storage, and the account-deletion function. Account identifiers, profiles, social relationships, scores, daily results, and synced progress are processed there.
  • Apple and Google provide optional sign-in. They give ANTICS the account information allowed by your provider settings. Apple may provide a private-relay email instead of your personal address.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the optional public support-request form. Information posted in a public issue is handled under GitHub’s terms and privacy policy, so do not post passwords, sign-in tokens, private email addresses, or other sensitive information.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Version 1.0 contains no advertising SDK and no analytics, attribution, or cross-app tracking SDK. ANTICS does not access the advertising identifier. If analytics are added later, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

Sharing and visibility

ANTICS may show a username, display name, avatar, rank, best score, or daily-result summary to other ANTICS users when a feature requires it and your privacy settings permit it. Raw private score payloads and email addresses are not exposed on public leaderboards. Accepted friends may see shared daily-result summaries when sharing is enabled.

When you tap a Share button, ANTICS creates text and opens the iOS share sheet. Apple and the destination you choose handle that share. ANTICS does not learn where you sent it.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you reset it, delete the app, or iOS removes the app’s storage. Synced account information remains while the account exists and as otherwise required for security or legal obligations.

To delete your ANTICS account, open You → Settings → Delete account. After the server confirms deletion, the account’s profile and linked synced records are deleted through database cascade rules, and the local session is removed. If you used Sign in with Apple, you may separately remove ANTICS from your Apple ID’s Sign in with Apple settings.

Security

ANTICS uses encrypted HTTPS connections, stores the native authentication session in iOS secure storage, and uses database access rules intended to limit account data to authorized users. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Children

ANTICS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you believe a child supplied account information, contact us through the support page so it can be reviewed and removed.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above will change, and material changes will be disclosed in the app or release notes when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the ANTICS support page. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, or sign-in tokens in a support request.
