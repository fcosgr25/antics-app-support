ANTICS support

Let’s get you back in the game.

Use the steps below for common problems. If they do not solve it, open a support request with your ANTICS version, iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

Open a support request View known issues

Support requests are public. Never include your password, Apple or Google sign-in codes, authentication tokens, private-relay address, or other sensitive personal information. You never need to post an email address for ordinary troubleshooting.

Quick fixes

The app will not open

Force-quit ANTICS, reopen it, and restart the iPhone if needed. Confirm the phone is running the supported iOS version and install the latest ANTICS update.

A game is stuck

Leave the game and start a new round. For a timed game, lock and unlock the phone once. If the problem repeats, include the game name and exact step in your report.

Scores are not syncing

Guest play works offline. For account sync, confirm you are signed in and online, then reopen the You tab. Do not reset local data while an unsynced score matters to you.

Haptics are missing

Check ANTICS Settings and the iPhone’s haptic settings. Some feedback can be reduced by system accessibility, power, or haptic preferences.

Delete your account

Open ANTICS → You → Settings → Delete account. Confirm the warning. The app only clears the signed-in account locally after the server confirms deletion.

If you signed in with Apple, you can also remove ANTICS from iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → ANTICS → Delete. Removing Apple access alone does not replace deleting the ANTICS account inside the app.

Reset guest data

Open ANTICS → You → Settings → Reset local data. This removes local names, pins, scores, history, daily progress, and achievements from that phone. The action cannot be undone. Signed-in data already synced to the account is not deleted by a local reset.

Privacy or safety issue

Use the support form and select Privacy or account deletion or Safety or abuse. Describe the issue without posting private credentials or sensitive information. You can also block another ANTICS account from the relevant social controls.

Before reporting a bug

  • Install the latest ANTICS version from the App Store or TestFlight.
  • Write down the game or utility name and the last button you pressed.
  • Include whether you were a guest, signed in, online, or offline.
  • Include your iPhone model and iOS version. A screenshot is helpful if it contains no private information.
